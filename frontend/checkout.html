To trigger a genuine decline / payment.failed webhook:
Card: 4000 0000 0000 0002 · Any future expiry · Any CVV

To trigger a genuine success / payment.captured webhook:
Card: 4111 1111 1111 1111 · Any future expiry · Any CVV

Both are official Razorpay test cards — the failure is a real bank-level decline in test mode, not something we're faking on our end.

What happens next

1. If the card declines, Razorpay sends a real payment.failed webhook to our backend.
2. Our backend marks the transaction failed and the AI agent classifies the failure automatically.
3. Go to the main dashboard — click "Run AI Recovery Agent" on that transaction to generate a recovery link.
4. Pay the recovery link with the success card — a real payment.captured webhook flips it to Recovered.
